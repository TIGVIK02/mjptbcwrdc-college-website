GRIEVANCE CELL

Student and staff grievance support

To address and resolve the grievances of students effectively, this cell is formed in the college.

The main objective of this cell is to encourage students to express their grievances and problems freely, without any fear of being victimized, and to ensure effective solutions to students' and staff grievances.

OBJECTIVES

OBJECTIVES

  • To develop a forum where students may voice their issues, both academic and non-academic.
  • To obtain feedback from students and take appropriate action for improvement in light of the feedback received.
  • The Grievance Cell is also empowered to look into matters of harassment.

MECHANISM OF REGISTERING GRIEVANCES

MEETING THE COMMITTEE

Students can express the problems they face by meeting the committee members in person.

SCOPE OF THE GRIEVANCES

SCOPE OF THE GRIEVANCES

ACADEMIC ISSUES

Issues related to:

  • Attendance
  • Assessment
  • Examinations
  • Extra-curricular and co-curricular activities

FINANCIAL ISSUES

Issues related to:

  • Infrastructure
  • Basic amenities
  • Sanitation

HARASSMENT AND RAGGING

Issues related to:

  • Harassment
  • Ragging by fellow students
  • Ragging or harassment by teachers
