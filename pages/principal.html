WELCOME TO STATION GHANPUR.

Dear students and parents, MJPTBCWRDC(W) is committed to quality higher education and a supportive residential environment for women students, especially from backward class communities.

Our aim is academic excellence together with self-confidence, leadership, employability skills, and personality development, so that every student can contribute as a responsible and empowered citizen.
