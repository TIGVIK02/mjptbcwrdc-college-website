HOW TO JOIN MJPTBCWRDC(W)

The college is a women’s residential degree college under MJPTBCWREIS, affiliated to Kakatiya University. Admission is conducted as per the society and university notifications issued each year.

Programmes currently offered: MPC, MPCs, MSCs, MECs, BZC, BA (EPH), and B.Com (BA).

For dates, eligibility, and allotment, please follow official MJPTBCWREIS / Kakatiya University circulars, or contact the college office.
