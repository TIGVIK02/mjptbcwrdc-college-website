ABOUT THE COLLEGE

MJPTBCWRDC(W), Station Ghanpur

Our purpose

MAHATMA JYOTHIBA PHULE TELANGANA BACKWARD CLASSES WELFARE RESIDENTIAL DEGREE COLLEGE FOR WOMEN

The college at Station Ghanpur, Pembarthy, Jangaon, is dedicated to providing quality higher education and residential facilities to women students, particularly from backward class communities.

The college promotes academic excellence, self-confidence, leadership, employability skills, and overall personality development, enabling students to become responsible and empowered members of society.

VISION

To create empowered women who excel in education, embrace opportunities, and contribute responsibly to the progress of society.

MISSION

  1. Educate with excellence
  2. Empower women with skills and confidence
  3. Nurture leadership and self-reliance
  4. Promote equality and social responsibility
  5. Build responsible and successful citizens
