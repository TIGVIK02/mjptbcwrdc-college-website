OFFICIAL COLLEGE IDENTITY

MJPTBCWRDC(W) is a women’s residential degree college at Station Ghanpur.

Mahatma Jyothiba Phule Telangana Backward Classes Welfare Residential Degree College for Women (MJPTBCWRDC(W)) is a women’s residential degree college located at Station Ghanpur, Pembarthy, Jangaon, Telangana. It is affiliated to Kakatiya University and serves students in a residential college environment with academic, welfare, and student-support programmes.

ABOUT THE COLLEGE

QUALITY HIGHER EDUCATION WITH RESIDENTIAL SUPPORT.

Mahatma Jyothiba Phule Telangana Backward Classes Welfare Residential Degree College for Women, Station Ghanpur · Women, is dedicated to providing quality higher education and residential facilities to women students, particularly from backward class communities.

The college promotes academic excellence, self-confidence, leadership, employability skills, and overall personality development, enabling students to become responsible and empowered members of society.

Vision & mission

OUR VISION

EMPOWERED WOMEN, RESPONSIBLE CITIZENS

To create empowered women who excel in education, embrace opportunities, and contribute responsibly to society.

OUR MISSION

EDUCATION WITH EXCELLENCE AND PURPOSE

Educate with excellence, nurture leadership and self-reliance, and promote equality and social responsibility.
